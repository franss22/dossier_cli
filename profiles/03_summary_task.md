# Task — Session Summary

Analyze this Delta Green: Impossible Landscapes tabletop RPG transcript and produce a detailed session summary.

The transcript contains:
- GM narration
- Player character dialogue
- Out-of-character discussion
- Rules/mechanical events

Prioritize understanding the events of the session over producing a simple transcript recap.

## Required Output

Produce the following sections:

# 1. Executive Summary

Provide a concise overview of the entire session.

Include:
- Main investigation developments
- Major discoveries
- Important character moments
- Overall direction of the story

Do not include speculation as fact.

---

# 2. Chronological Timeline

Create a timeline of important events.

For each entry include:
- Timestamp in HH:MM format
- What happened
- Relevant characters involved

Focus on:
- Scene transitions
- Discoveries
- Conversations with important NPCs
- Major decisions
- Supernatural events
- Mechanical events

Ignore small talk and irrelevant conversation unless it contributes to character development or atmosphere.

---

# 3. Investigation Summary

Separate information into:

## Confirmed Facts

Things directly established by:
- GM narration
- NPC statements
- Successful investigation results

## Player Theories

Ideas proposed by players that are not confirmed.

Clearly mark these as theories.

## Unresolved Mysteries

Questions that remain unanswered at the end of the session.

---

# 4. NPC Summary

For each important NPC appearing in the session:

Include:
- Name
- Role in the story
- Important information revealed
- Relationship to the agents
- Changes in status or behavior

---

# 5. Character Summary

For each player character:

Include:
- Important actions taken
- Information discovered
- Decisions made
- Character moments
- Consequences or changes affecting them

---

# 6. Locations

List important locations visited or discussed.

Include:
- Events that occurred there
- Important clues
- Changes or discoveries

---

# 7. Mechanical Events

Track relevant Delta Green mechanics:

Include:
- Skill checks
- SAN checks
- Willpower checks
- Failed or successful rolls
- Mechanical consequences

Do not include every minor roll unless it affected the story.

---

# 8. New Leads and Unresolved Threads

Create a list of investigation threads to follow in future sessions.

Include:
- Lead/question
- Current information
- Possible next steps

---

# 9. Memorable Moments

Highlight:
- Horror moments
- Important reveals
- Funny player moments
- Strong roleplaying scenes
- Important quotes

---

## Analysis Guidelines

- Use canonical names from the normalization rules.
- Use character names rather than player names when describing in-game actions.
- Preserve uncertainty: do not turn player speculation into confirmed facts.
- Distinguish between what characters know and what players know.
- Preserve the tone of Delta Green: paranoia, uncertainty, and investigation.
- Use timestamps formatted as HH:MM:SS, not raw seconds.
- Write the summary in Spanish.
- Keep English proper nouns and RPG terminology in English.
